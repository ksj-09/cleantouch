# PC recognition precision — development preview

The preview rejects background-dominated classifications and undersized crops. It compares product and surrounding crops; uncertain or conflicting candidates are checked by the existing operator Gemini endpoint. Only high/medium responses with a usable product label are accepted. Unavailable verification excludes the candidate rather than inventing a name.

Confident coloured lightsabers require both a glowing shaft and a matching local AI classification. Unknown glowing regions (including subtitles and skin) are excluded. Smaller verified-looking glowing candidates have a separate size threshold so distant props remain visible.

Repeated candidates are compared by image embedding, name, overlap and video identity. Different sword colours and separate objects in the same frame remain separate. This is similarity-based merging, not proof that two products are identical.

Automatic remote checks are bounded to 8 per scan session and 4 per frame, spaced at least 6.5 seconds apart. A failure backs off for 60 seconds. Repeated candidate embeddings reuse prior answers. Only candidate images are transmitted; users do not supply keys. Unconfirmed omissions are reported, and manual AI selection remains available.

Validation: 11 policy/label/glow tests passed; chatbot/selection integration passed. The real lightsaber scene yields exactly red, green and purple lightsabers, without extra text/face/window candidates. A live Gemini bag fixture yielded a black bag strap, merged across two sightings; point segmentation yielded a bag crop. These fixtures do not establish a general accuracy percentage.

Changes are in source/development preview; published 0.1.28 installers and Android 0.4.8 do not contain this update. Close and reopen the desktop development preview to load it.

## Whole-product follow-up

Detector reconciliation now prefers complete full-frame observations to clipped tile fragments. Bag candidates use point segmentation, with person head/feet and distant torso points excluded. Crops whose masks reach an artificial tile edge retry in a wider region. Successful bag cut-outs are used directly for cards and searches, cached for repeated scenes, and composited on white for classification and remote verification.

The live bag fixture now produces one black bag result (two sightings) instead of strap fragments. Point selection retains the bag body and excludes the wearer face; the exported mask is 264 × 441 instead of the previous 296 × 694 person silhouette. Local classification returns bag after transparent-image compositing. Some surrounding clothing remains attached to this mask: this update improves whole-product coverage but does not establish clean object-only contours in all cases. New three region-selection tests bring policy coverage to 14 passing tests.

## 2026-10-07: local rolling capture + multi-frame AI
The prior Gemini Live path is replaced by local capture (target 10 samples/sec), a 6-second / 60-frame / 24-MiB memory buffer, and ordinary Gemini requests on user questions. No cloud request on opening sharing. Up to six chronological frames including a freshly captured current scene are sent only on a question. Last frame is explicitly marked current in model instructions. Sampling skips while encoding, scanning paused, video unlinked, or obstructed. Reset/end clears history; video identity change discards prior frames. Samples at max 960px, AI input at max 768px. Actual frame rate depends on device and renderer load.
Persistent analysis scenes are now collected every second using the sharpest/most changed sample from the last second.
